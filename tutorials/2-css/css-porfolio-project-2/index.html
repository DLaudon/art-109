About Me

Derek Laudon is an engineering student at SJSU, adopting artificial intelligence, made my real sketches, linework, smudges and erased mistakes fed to artificial intelligence diffusion and iterated and reiterated push back against it till a result that neither separate could produce. People ask is machine art, art?. But that question was thrown at the history of art, a single color painting or a banana taped to a wall. If that can be called art, it was not about how, but the emotion, idea, and intent that comes from it. Using a four sketch concept incomplete, merging to a comprehensive design, from my exact words to actuality.
